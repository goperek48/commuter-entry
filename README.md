One line of weather for a city that does not exist, written for make.fun's "Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't" bounty ($2). A bored commuter at a rainy bus stop reads it and smiles.

## The line

> Good morning, Nimbleville: light drizzle of confetti, winds at a gentle 12 mph, and a 90% chance your umbrella will be stolen by a seagull.

139 characters. Nimbleville appears on no map; the forecast keeps the rhythm of broadcast weather — greeting, conditions, wind, a percentage — and bends it once, with confetti for rain and a seagull after the commuter's umbrella.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly one line, no trailing newline.
